## 3. Keyword Placement & On-Page Implementation Rules

Har publication ke liye in 6 rules ko follow karein:

1. **Title Tag:** Primary target keyword title ke pehle 60 characters mein shamil ho (e.g. `How to Improve INP: A Senior Engineer's Field Guide | Readora`).
2. **Main `<h1>`:** Sirf 1 H1 per page, jo target keyword ko natural conversational tone mein include kare.
3. **Pehle 100 Words (Introductory Paragraph):** Keyword ya uska LSI variation pehle paragraph mein naturally appear ho.
4. **Semantic Subheadings (`<h2>` & `<h3>`):** Related long-tail questions (e.g., *"What is a good INP score?"*, *"How does LCP affect bounce rate?"*).
5. **Clean Keyword URL:** Short, slugified, hyphens-only URL without dates or numbers (e.g., `/blog/how-to-improve-inp`).
6. **Schema & FAQ Matching:** FAQ questions mein direct search query phrasing use karein taake Google `FAQPage` rich accordion snippet display kare.

## 5. 10-Step Safe & High-Authority Backlink Plan (Free & White-Hat)

### 1. Developer Platform Syndication (Dev.to, Hashnode, Medium)
* **Strategy:** Readora ke articles ka summary ya adapted version Dev.to aur Hashnode par publish karein.
* **Canonical Tag:** Post ke bottom par likhein: *"Originally published with full benchmarks at [Readora](https://readora.me/blog/...)"* aur platform ke settings mein **Canonical URL** Readora ka link set karein.
* **Result:** Google canonical authority Readora ko transfer karta hai aur developer traffic aati hai.

### 2. Journalist PR Outreach (Connectively / HARO / Qwoted / Featured.com)
* **Strategy:** Qwoted aur Connectively par free journalist accounts banayein.
* **Action:** Reporters daily sawal poochte hain: *"Looking for a web performance engineer to explain Core Web Vitals"* ya *"Cybersecurity consultant needed for advice on password managers"*.
* **Result:** Jab aap 2-3 paragraph ka concise technical answer denge, to Forbes, TechRadar, Business Insider, ya zapier se **DA 80+ natural editorial backlinks** milte hain.

### 3. Community Engagement (Reddit, Hacker News, Indie Hackers)
* **Subreddits:** `r/webdev`, `r/nextjs`, `r/artificial`, `r/cybersecurity`, `r/SideProject`.
* **Strategy:** Direct link post na karein (spam flag ho jayega). Post mein **poori case study ya value** text format mein share karein. Comments mein jab log further details poochein ya credits ke tor par article link dein: *"Full architectural breakdown & benchmarks documented here: [readora.me/...]"*.

### 4. High-Intent Quora & Stack Overflow Answers
* **Strategy:** Un sawalon ko target karein jo Google search mein top 3 par aate hain (e.g. *"What is Interaction to Next Paint in Core Web Vitals?"*).
* **Action:** Ek comprehensive, high-quality code explanation likhein aur reference link Readora article ka dein.

### 5. Curated Resource Page Link Building
* **Strategy:** Google par search karein:
  * `intitle:"resources" "web development"`
  * `intitle:"useful links" "cybersecurity guide"`
  * `"awesome-nextjs" github`
* **Action:** Site owners ko email karein:
  > *"Hi [Name], I noticed your curated list of web performance resources on [Page URL]. We recently published an in-depth, peer-cited field guide breaking down 2026 Core Web Vitals diagnostics. Thought it might be a valuable addition for your readers: [Readora URL]. Keep up the great work!"*

### 6. Broken Link Building
* **Strategy:** Ahrefs Free Broken Link Checker ya Check My Links extension se industry blogs ke 404 links dhoondein.
* **Action:** Webmaster ko inform karein ke unki site par broken link hai aur Readora ka live, updated guide replacement ke tor par recommend karein.

### 7. Original Research & Benchmark Data
* **Strategy:** Chhoti original research share karein (e.g. *"Next.js 14 App Router vs Pages Router: Real-World INP Test Across 50 Sites"*).
* **Result:** Technical writers aur bloggers statistics aur benchmark data ko quote karke **automatically bina maange backlink** dete hain.

### 8. Reputable Tech & Startup Directories
* Apni publication ko free trustworthy directories par register karein:
  * **Product Hunt** (Launch as a digital publication / developer journal)
  * **AlternativeTo** (Alternative to Medium / Substack)
  * **Indie Hackers Product Directory**
  * **GitHub Topics / Awesome Lists** (Submit pull request to relevant Awesome-Web-Performance or Awesome-AI lists)

### 9. Social Amplification (LinkedIn & X / Twitter)
* **Strategy:** Technical threads likhein (e.g. *"5 things most developers get wrong about Interaction to Next Paint 🧵"*).
* **Result:** Viral reach se tech founders aur journalists tak baat pahunchti hai jo articles ko apne newsletters (e.g., TLDR, JavaScript Weekly) mein feature karte hain.

### 10. Technical Podcasts & Webinars
* Tech podcasts par guest speaker ban kar software architecture ya AI workflows par baat karein. Show notes mein Readora ka permanent dofollow backlink milta hai.

## 6. Strict Anti-Patterns (Kya Hargiz Nahi Karna)

> [!CAUTION] Google SpamBrain & Link Spam Updates Penalty Risk
> In 4 cheezon se 100% door rahein:
> 1. **Fiverr / Sasti Backlink Packages:** "Buy 5,000 backlinks for \$10" — yeh 100% spammy link farms hoti hain jo Google search index se site ko ban karwa sakti hain.
> 2. **PBN (Private Blog Networks):** Fake blogs ke networks jo sirf link pass karne ke liye banaye jate hain.
> 3. **Automated Comment Spam:** Blogs ya forums par bots ke zariye comments mein links chhorna.
> 4. **Exact-Match Anchor Over-Optimization:** Agar 100 backlinks mein se 90 ka anchor text exact `"how to improve inp"` hoga to Google algorithmic penalty lagata hai. Anchor text natural, branded (`Readora`, `Ali Raza`), ya generic (`source`, `this guide`) hona chahiye.
